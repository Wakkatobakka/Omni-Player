OMNI PLAYER / DIGITAL MENU VISUALS

Direction: late-1990s / early-2000s game menus, chunky pixels, navy and
cobalt panels, cyan edges and orange selection accents.

Hero artwork was generated with AI from the approved Omni visual concept.
The gameplay strip contains unaltered recorded frames from Wakkatobakka's
public gameplay hub: Resident Evil: Genesis (BREW), FFVII Snowboarding
(DoJa), and Sonic Jump (DoJa). Screens are fitted with their aspect ratios
preserved. No generated game scenes are used in the finished README.
